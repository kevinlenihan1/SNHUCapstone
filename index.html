Hi there and welcome to my Computer Science ePortfolio! This is where I showcase all the knowledge, skills, and growth I’ve accomplished during my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University. The artifacts included in this portfolio focus on three key areas of computer science: software design and engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases. Included in each section are links to the original assignment I submitted as well as an enhanced version where I went back and redesigned the program to showcase what I’ve learned since making the original. When I joined this program in October 2022, I barely knew anything about computer science. Since then I have gained significant experience in developing applications, database design, system architecture, and secure coding practices. Through the enhancements presented here, I highlight the learning process and growth that has allowed me to transform my early work into more effective professional solutions.
